title: 26.3 Update
---

OGCraft will be updating to 26.3 on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 8:30 AM MDT.

The overworld will be expanded by 2.5k blocks making the world border now 15k by 15k blocks. The new poplar biome will be available in the newly expanded areas.

The seasonal Underground Crate will be replaced with the brand new seasonal Autumn Crate. Players will be able to exchange any Underground keys for Autumn keys and get the chance to win new and unique items and vouchers.

We hope everyone enjoys this new update and, as always, let us know if anything is not working properly in [#bug-reports](https://discord.com/channels/724002762320380015/1141655676326772827)

-OGCraft Staff Team
