Hello @everyone

We have some exciting news to share but first the senior staff team would like to address an item that was included in one of our crates. This item was added with the intention to be a joke but we have now learned that the item had an additional meaning that we missed prior to adding it to the crate. This was an honest mistake with no ill intentions. We recognize that it shouldn’t have been included in the first place, and we apologize for the oversight.

The item has now been removed, and we will be more mindful moving forward to make sure the content we add reflects the kind of community we want to build. We appreciate those who brought this to our attention and apologize to anyone who was affected by the item. We will be removing the item and replacing it with a key key to compensate those who were affected.

Now onto the exciting part...
We would now like to announce that OGCraft will be updating to 26.3 on Saturday, October 24, 2026, at 8:30 AM MDT!! The server will be down from 8:30 AM to 10:30 AM while we get everything updated and ready to go! Massive shoutout to OJ and their team of developers for pulling out all the stops.

The overworld will be expanded by 2.5k blocks making our world border now 15k by 15k blocks. The new poplar biome will be available in the newly expanded areas.

We will also be replacing the seasonal Underground Crate with the brand new Autumn Crate! You'll be able to exchange any Underground keys for Autumn keys and get the chance to win some new and unique items as well as plenty of vouchers!

We hope you all enjoy this new update and, as always, if you notice anything not working properly let us know in [#bug-reports](https://discord.com/channels/724002762320380015/1141655676326772827)

-OGCraft Staff Team
