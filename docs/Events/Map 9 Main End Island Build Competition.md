# End Island Build Competition

**In the END, anything is possible**

Hello all! Now that things are kicked off with map 9, we feel that a building competition is in order! Lets show off those build you can create! 

This competition will run from **9th June - 9th July**

As we know the end island is looking for a style and a build to cement itself as the definitive OGCraft end island, this is your chance to show off what your vision for the end is! Whether you build solo or choose to team up, your imaginations is your limit, whether you want to make a dark fantasy dragons den or a sci-fi build that makes you seek end adventure, its truly in your hands to show off what you can do. Due to there only being one end island on OGCraft we are allowing builds off the server to be submitted.

**Just remember that the dragon needs to be respawned and the obsidian pillars mined, please make sure this is possible in your designs/builds**.

You can submit your design entry to the discord thread and find the schematic of the end island [here](https://discord.com/channels/724002762320380015/1513965095456080022).

#### Prizes:

**1st Place:** 
You get your build to be the OGCraft end island! You can build it on the server!
Additionally you/your team all get a 1 month wither voucher each, as well as key bundle each (5 tools and gear + 5 mega keys).

**2nd Place:** 
You/your team all get a 1 month golem voucher each, as well as key bundle each (3 tools and gear + 3 mega keys).

**3rd Place:** 
You/your team all get a 1 month allay voucher each, as well as key bundle each (1 tools and gear + 1 mega key).


#### Judging notice:
This competition will be judged by the OGCraft staff team, this will take place over the course of a week once the submission time is up.

Upon completion of competition:  
If you get 1st place you and your team get to build your build in the OGCraft end! If the project gets abandoned the second place entry will get the chance to build thiers.

#### Rules:
- Maximum team size of 4 Players
- All builds must follow the OGCraft rules 
- All builds that are submitted must be original (This will be checked)
- Any late submissions will not be judged and your submission will be invalid
- Staff are not to be messaged regarding judging or your finishing place. All decisions are final.
